Objective: This document identifies 5 essential features that should be present on the InAmigos Foundation website. Each section includes an annotated visual layout and implementation notes explaining why the feature matters for an NGO website.
Feature 1 💚 Donation Section with Hero CTA
Hero Banner + Embedded Donation Widget
The homepage hero should combine an emotional message with a frictionless donation experience — goal progress, preset amounts, and a single-click payment path.

🎯 Why This Feature Matters
A compelling hero with an embedded donation widget reduces friction — visitors can donate in one click without navigating away. Quick-amount buttons (₹500, ₹1000, ₹2000) paired with a live progress bar create urgency and social proof. Currently, the InAmigos site redirects to Razorpay externally; an embedded widget on the homepage can significantly boost conversion rates.
Feature 2 📊 Impact Stats & Showcase
Animated Impact Numbers & Fund Breakdown
Real numbers build donor trust. A visual stats section shows exactly where contributions go, reinforced by certifications and project-wise fund distribution.

🎯 Why This Feature Matters
Animated counters and project-wise fund distribution charts make donors feel accountable and proud. InAmigos holds 80G, 12A, ISO 9001:2015, NITI Aayog, and CSR-1 certifications — displaying these as trust badges next to the impact numbers dramatically improves donor confidence and helps close first-time donations.
Feature 3 🙌 Volunteer Sign-Up Form
Embedded In-Page Volunteer Registration
An easy, branded in-page volunteer form lowers the barrier for community participation and eliminates the drop-off caused by external redirects.

🎯 Why This Feature Matters
Currently, the volunteer button on InAmigos redirects to an external Google Form. An embedded, branded form keeps users on-site, improves completion rates, and allows the foundation to store data directly. Interest tagging also enables the team to match volunteers to the right projects (Jeev, Udaan, Prakriti, etc.) automatically.

Timely campaigns and a filterable events calendar keep donors and volunteers engaged throughout the year and create urgency to act.

🎯 Why This Feature Matters
A dynamic campaign tracker with countdowns and seat limits creates urgency. A filterable events calendar (by cause category) helps supporters find relevant opportunities. Campaigns linked to specific projects (Prakriti, Jeev, Udaan) help donors contribute to causes they care about most — and an RSVP system builds a pipeline of engaged volunteers for each event.
Feature 5 💬 Testimonials & Social Proof
Beneficiary, Volunteer & Donor Stories
Stories with photos, names, and ratings add authenticity. A well-designed testimonials section covers all audience types and addresses each person's specific motivation for engaging.

🎯 Why This Feature Matters
Testimonials with photos/avatars, a name, location, and star rating add credibility and authenticity. A rotating carousel or 2-column grid covering beneficiaries, volunteers, and corporate donors addresses all three audience types visiting the site. Social proof is one of the strongest conversion drivers for NGO websites — visitors who see real stories are far more likely to donate or volunteer.
